The [suite README](https://github.com/BugTraceAI/BugTraceAI#readme) explains the components, architecture and available workflows. Each component's README covers its installation and configuration.

## Questions and troubleshooting

Ask installation, configuration and workflow questions in [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/BugTraceAI/BugTraceAI/discussions). Include the affected product and version, operating system, and local or Docker setup.

For a reproducible defect, open an issue in the affected repository. Include concise reproduction steps and sanitized logs. Features and integration proposals can also be discussed there.

The [Discord community](https://discord.gg/5HjujkScC) is another place for product discussion and feedback.

## Sensitive reports

For vulnerabilities in BugTraceAI itself, follow the repository's security policy or the [organization reporting guide](https://github.com/BugTraceAI/.github/blob/main/SECURITY.md). Keep credentials and private target evidence out of public issues and discussions.
